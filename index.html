About Us

Who we are.

Tunga & Funga Real Estate Agency and Property Management Firm handles property sales, rentals, and management across Kano and Nigeria.

We started Tunga & Funga to bring one consistent name to the buying, renting, and managing of property in Kano and across Nigeria — handled the way it's always been done locally, but with a clearer front door for clients.

The company began operation in 2020, with the sole purpose of ensuring transparent and verified real estate transactions in the Jaba–Panisau and Ungogo axis of Kano State, Nigeria. In 5 years, the company has served clients across Nigeria and processed over 1,000 transactions for satisfied clients from all over the country.

Every real estate deal we handle follows a rigorous vetting and verification process, because we value the integrity of the deal, the trust of the client, and the satisfaction that comes with helping our clients reach their real estate goals. We're here to guide you and help you avoid traps and bad deals when investing in real estate.
